GSync Privacy Policy

Last updated: 11 September 2026

The short version. GSync runs entirely on your Mac. It has no servers, no accounts and no analytics. Nothing it reads from Google Drive is ever sent to the developer or to any third party. The only network connections it makes are to Google's own APIs, on your behalf, using credentials you created yourself.

Who this covers

GSync is a personal, non-commercial macOS application developed by Tatsuya Yamashita ("the developer"). This policy describes how the application handles your information. It applies to the GSync application only.

What GSync accesses

When you sign in, GSync asks Google for read-only access to your Drive (https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive.readonly). With that access it reads:

  • the names, identifiers, sizes, checksums, modification times and folder structure of files in the Drive folders you choose to sync;
  • the contents of those files, in order to download copies to the local folder you chose;
  • your account's e-mail address, display name, profile picture and storage usage, so the app can show you which account is connected.

GSync never requests write access. It cannot create, modify, move or delete anything in your Google Drive.

Where that information goes

It stays on your Mac. The developer operates no servers and receives none of it. Specifically:

  • Downloaded files are written to the local folder you chose.
  • File metadata (names, Drive identifiers, sizes, checksums, timestamps, local paths and sync bookkeeping) is stored in a local database at ~/Library/Application Support/GSync/.
  • Sign-in tokens and the OAuth client ID and secret you enter are stored in your macOS login keychain, protected by the operating system.
  • Activity logs are written to the local database and to the macOS unified log on your Mac, for troubleshooting.

GSync contains no analytics, no crash reporting, no advertising identifiers and no third-party SDKs. It makes no network connections other than to Google's authentication and Drive API endpoints.

Limited Use disclosure

GSync's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs to any other app will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Data obtained from Google Drive is used solely to provide the syncing feature you asked for, is not transferred to anyone else except as necessary to provide that feature, is not used for advertising, and is not read by humans.

Your OAuth client

GSync does not ship with shared credentials. You create your own OAuth client in your own Google Cloud project and paste it into the app, so the connection between GSync and Google exists only between your Mac and your own Google Cloud project.

Removing your data

  • Choose Sign Out in GSync. This revokes the token with Google and deletes it from your keychain.
  • Review or remove the app's access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
  • Delete ~/Library/Application Support/GSync/ to remove the local sync database.
  • Delete the application. Files already downloaded to your own folders are yours and are left alone.

Children

GSync is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect information from them.
